AYM App Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

Area Youth Ministry ("AYM", "we", "us") makes the AYM app and the AYM check-in website so students can check in to AYM events. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Who the app is for

The app is for students aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

What we collect

  • When you sign up or check in: your phone number, name, gender, school level and class year, school name, school borough, and home borough.
  • Each time you check in: the event, the date and time, and that you agreed to the AYM Code of Conduct.
  • From your check-ins: we calculate attendance totals, streaks, points and levels.
  • Added by AYM leaders: leaders may add notes to your record about your involvement with AYM, including whether you have shared that you are a Christian.

The app uses your camera only to read event QR codes on your phone. We do not collect, store or send photos or video. We do not collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and the app has no ads.

The app saves your phone number and name on your device so it can check you in faster next time. Tap "Not you?" in the app to remove them.

How we use it

  • To check you in to events and keep attendance records for AYM events and groups.
  • To show your streaks, points and levels.
  • To help AYM leaders follow up with students and plan events.
  • To send you text messages about AYM events, if you have opted in. Reply STOP to any text to stop them.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information or share it for advertising. Only AYM leaders who manage events can see student records. We use these service providers, which handle information only to provide their services to AYM:

  • Supabase: database and app back end.
  • Cloudflare: website hosting.
  • Clearstream: text messages to students who opt in.
  • Bolo Forms: sign-up and registration forms for AYM events.
  • Google: Google Sheets, for event attendance lists, shared only with authorized AYM leaders; and Google Gemini, an AI assistant that helps leaders review attendance.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of students and others.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you take part in AYM and for 4 years after your last check-in, then delete it. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your information, email philip.sun@acts.network. We will respond within 30 days. Parents and guardians can make these requests for their child.

Security

Your information is stored with access controls so that only authorized AYM leaders can see it. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: philip.sun@acts.network.
